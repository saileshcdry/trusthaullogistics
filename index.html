We Only Move 4-Wheelers. That's the Point.

General transporters move cement, furniture, scrap — and your vehicle. We don't. Every job is a car, SUV, jeep or passenger van, handled by drivers who know vehicles, and documented end-to-end.

✅ What we drive

  • Hatchbacks and sedans
  • SUVs and crossover vehicles
  • Jeeps and double-cab pickups
  • Passenger vans (Hiace, Winger)
  • EVs and hybrids
  • New (unregistered) vehicles
  • Used (registered) vehicles
  • Single vehicles and batches

✕ What we don't

  • Two-wheelers, bikes, scooters
  • Buses and minibuses
  • Trucks and heavy commercial vehicles

  • Household or general cargo
  • Cross-border / import clearance
  • Vehicles outside Nepal
