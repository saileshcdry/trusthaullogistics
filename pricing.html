What's Included — and What's Not

No surprise line items. One price, everything covered.

✅ Included in the quote

  • 13% VAT
  • Fuel for the full journey
  • Highway and bridge tolls
  • Driver return journey cost
  • Transit insurance
  • Pickup and delivery photos
  • Odometer record at pickup and delivery
  • Signed delivery receipt

✕ Not included

  • Bluebook transfer fees (if any)
  • Vehicle registration taxes
  • Municipal parking charges at destination
  • Overnight stays if transit exceeds published days (rare; agreed upfront)
  • Charges for special handling requests (agreed before booking)
