Our 4-Wheeler Drive-Away Services

Four services, one standard: vetted drivers, photos and odometer at every handover, transit insurance, and fuel/tolls included in one NPR price.

🏢

Dealership → Dealership Transfers

Stock movement between your showrooms and dealer network — scheduled, documented, and billed once a month.

Ideal for dealers balancing inventory across Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan and Terai locations. Book a standing route (e.g. every Tuesday) and let us handle the driver logistics while your team focuses on selling.

What's included
  • Standing weekly or bi-weekly routes
  • Multi-vehicle batches with one driver per vehicle
  • Consolidated monthly invoice across all runs
  • Driver name and phone shared before pickup
  • Photos + odometer archived per vehicle
  • Fuel, tolls, driver return included
🏠

Dealership → Customer Doorstep Delivery

Last-mile delivery to your buyer's address by a vetted professional driver.

Your customer gets a clean, on-time delivery — your showroom gets the credit. We coordinate directly with your dealership on scheduling and with the customer on arrival, so nothing strains your sales team's time.

What's included
  • Door-to-door — driver comes to the customer's address
  • Photo inspection + odometer at pickup
  • Photo inspection + odometer at delivery
  • Signed delivery receipt returned to your dealership
  • Bluebook / ownership paperwork handed to customer
  • WhatsApp updates to both dealership and customer
🚙

Single Vehicle & Premium Drive-Away

One vehicle, one dedicated driver, pickup to delivery — no handoffs mid-route.

Ideal for premium cars, EVs, and first-owner deliveries. The same driver who picks up your vehicle delivers it — no mid-route driver swaps, no shared journeys with strangers.

What's included
  • Dedicated driver pickup to delivery
  • Odometer trail documented end-to-end
  • Fuel, tolls, driver return included
  • Daily WhatsApp check-ins with location
  • Insurance certificate shared before transit
  • Route plan agreed with you upfront
🚐

Passenger Vans, Jeeps & Pickups

Hiace, Winger, jeeps and double-cab pickups driven by experienced highway drivers.

Larger vehicles need drivers who know Nepal's highways and handle them with care. Same transit insurance and photo-verification as every other load — no mixed cargo, no shortcuts.

What's included
  • Highway-experienced driver for larger vehicles
  • Same insurance + photo verification as all jobs
  • Fuel, tolls, driver return included
  • Daily WhatsApp check-ins during transit
  • Route planning for wide/oversize vehicles
  • Delivery to dealership or customer, your choice

How It Works

Four steps. You know who is driving your vehicle and where it is at every stage.

1

Book & Confirm

Quote confirmed on WhatsApp or phone. Pickup date, route and driver locked before anything moves.

2

Driver Handover at Pickup

Vetted driver arrives. Photos of all four sides plus odometer recorded. Fuel confirmed. Signed by both sides.

3

Transit

Driver follows the agreed route with daily WhatsApp check-ins and location updates. No unauthorised detours.

4

Delivery & Handover

Photos and odometer taken again at delivery, delivery receipt signed, paperwork handed to dealership or owner.

What We Move — and What We Don't

If your vehicle doesn't fit the list below, call us — we'll tell you straight.

✅ We drive

  • Hatchbacks and sedans

  • Jeeps and double-cab pickups
