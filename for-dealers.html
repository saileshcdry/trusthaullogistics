Dealer-Specific Questions

The ones your inventory manager asks us on the first call.

Can you handle 20 vehicles in a week?

Yes. We organise multi-vehicle batches with one driver per vehicle. For 20+ vehicles in a single week we'd typically run 2–3 scheduled batches. Tell us the volume upfront and we'll quote a batch rate and confirm the schedule in writing.

How does consolidated billing work?

You receive one invoice per calendar month covering all runs, with vehicle lists, route details, pickup/delivery dates and VAT breakdown. Payment terms are agreed at onboarding — standard is 15-day from invoice date. We can also accommodate per-run invoicing if your accounting prefers it.

What's your damage policy?

Photos and odometer readings are taken at pickup and at delivery, signed by both sides. If damage occurs in our care, you file a claim with our transit insurer and we support it with pickup and delivery photos, odometer records, route logs and driver statements. You do not need to prove the damage happened — our records do that.

Do you share driver details before pickup?

Yes. Every driver's name, phone and licence are shared with you before the vehicle leaves. Drivers are vetted, background-checked and briefed on the specific route and any special handling requirements you flag.

Can you do same-day pickups?

Yes, subject to driver availability — usually within 4–6 hours for Kathmandu Valley routes, longer for out-of-valley. Same-day bookings should go directly to WhatsApp or phone, not the form.
