Owner Questions

The ones first-time customers always ask.

Will my car be driven by a professional?

Yes. Every driver is licence-verified, background checked, and has a reviewed driving record. We share the driver's name and phone before pickup. For single-vehicle bookings the same driver stays with your vehicle pickup to delivery — no handoffs.

How do I know the driver doesn't detour?

Every driver follows the agreed route and checks in daily with location on WhatsApp. The odometer is recorded at pickup and delivery, and shared with you — so any unexplained distance is visible. No unauthorised detours are tolerated.

What if something scratches the car?

We photograph every side of the vehicle at pickup, so any pre-existing scratches are documented before the vehicle moves. If new damage occurs in transit, you file a claim with our transit insurer and we support it with pickup and delivery photos, odometer records and driver statements.

Do you deliver to my home?

Yes. Door-to-door — driver comes to your pickup address and delivers to your delivery address. No depot drop-offs. The only exception is if a narrow street physically blocks access; in that case we agree a safe nearby handover point with you in advance.

How long does delivery take?

Within Kathmandu Valley: same day or next day. Kathmandu to Pokhara or Chitwan: 1–2 days. Kathmandu to Birgunj, Biratnagar or Nepalgunj: 2–3 days. Timelines assume normal conditions — we tell you upfront if monsoon or highway closures might add time.
